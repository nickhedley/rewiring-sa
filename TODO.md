# Rewiring South Africa – to-do list

## Ideas to assess

1. A one-stop shop for home electrification (added 16 Oct 2026).
   - Precedent: Australia's federal government is funding a trial through ARENA (A$8.2 million), run by
     Electric Future with Rewiring Australia's support, for up to 1,000 households in southern Canberra. One app
     covers advice, fixed-price quotes, installer matching, scheduling, quality checks, rebate processing and
     after-sales support. Most households get a 20% rebate on installation and 3% finance on the first
     A$20,000; concession card holders 0% on the first A$10,000. Source: RenewEconomy, 'Government backs trial
     of one-stop shop to make home electrification less daunting for households', 17 Sep 2026.
   - South African scope to work out: which upgrades (electric geyser to heat pump, gas to induction, rooftop
     solar and battery), who installs and how they are vetted, how it is financed, and which rebates, tariffs
     and registration steps apply.
   - Related: Eskom dropped the professional engineer sign-off for small-scale embedded generation connected
     to its grid in October 2025, which GreenCape estimates saves about R9,000 per installation (GreenCape,
     National Renewable Energy Market Intelligence Report 2026, p.8). Cloover (Germany) sells only through
     independent installers with financing at the point of sale (Tech.eu, 1 Sep 2026).
